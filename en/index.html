Fabiano Santos

Full Professor at the Institute for Social and Political Studies of the Rio de Janeiro State University (IESP-UERJ). He holds a PhD and an MA in Political Science from IUPERJ and was a postdoctoral fellow at the University of California, San Diego.

His research addresses the internal organisation of the Brazilian National Congress — standing committees, agenda power, party discipline — and executive–legislative relations under coalitional presidentialism. Since 2018 he has also worked on the political economy of legislative decision-making and on political representation in the Legal Amazon.

He has served as president of the Brazilian Political Science Association and of the National Association for Graduate Studies and Research in the Social Sciences. He is an associate editor of the Journal of Politics in Latin America and coordinates six research groups at IESP-UERJ.
